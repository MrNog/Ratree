# Ratree

**Talents and glyphs for World of Warcraft 3.3.5a, in one window.**

Plan every point first, learn them all at once. No more misclicks during a respec.

## What it does

- **Plan, then learn.** Left click adds a point, right click takes it back. Nothing is spent until you
  press **Learn Talents (N)**, which shows how many points are planned. **Discard** throws the plan away.
- **Presets.** Save a build ("Frost DK", "Blood Tank") and apply it later, for example on a freshly
  reset dual spec. Presets are kept per class and shared by every character of that class.
- **Both specs.** Browse your other spec and switch to it from the footer.
- **Hunter pets.** The pet tree has its own tab.
- **Glyphs.** Three major and three minor sockets on a gold star, each showing the icon of the ability it
  changes, with every active glyph listed on the right.
- **Rat art.** The rat of the spec you have the most points in sits behind the trees.

## Use

- The talent key (N), the glyph key and the talent micro button open Ratree instead of the default window.
- `/ratree` opens it, `/ratree glyphs` opens the glyph page, `/ratree scale 0.85` resizes it.
- Turn off other talent addons (Talented, etc.) so they don't fight over the window.

## Notes

- WotLK cannot take back a learned talent point. To apply a preset over a different build, reset your
  talents at the class trainer first.
- Talents can't be learned in combat.
